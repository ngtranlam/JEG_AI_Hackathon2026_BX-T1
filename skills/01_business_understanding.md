# Skill 01 — Business Understanding

## Challenge

BX-T1 requires a **Content Creation Video Factory**.

The expected outcome is not just one generated video.

The system must act like a production pipeline:

```text
Brief
→ content plan
→ creative variants
→ production
→ post-production
→ export
```

## User pain

Creators and small teams spend 8–20 hours producing one short-form video.

The system should reduce production time by automating:

```text
Hook generation
Script writing
Storyboard planning
Video generation
Voiceover
Subtitles
Caption
Cover
Export formats
```

## User groups

```text
Solo creators
In-house content teams
SME marketing managers
E-commerce brands
F&B brands
Service businesses
```

## Required output

At least:

```text
1 video publish-ready
2 creative variants
9:16 export
1:1 export
Reusable TRAE workflow template
```

Recommended output:

```text
Variant A 9:16
Variant A 1:1
Variant B 9:16
Variant B 1:1
Cover images
Captions
Titles
Hashtags
Scripts
Storyboards
Evaluation reports
```

## What makes the product strong

```text
- Clear TRAE orchestration
- Seedance 2.0 usage is obvious
- 2 variants are meaningfully different
- Video is close to publish-ready
- UI is simple but polished
- Workflow can be reused with a new brief
- Handles Seedance duration limit via segmentation
```

## How judges may evaluate

Likely judging areas:

```text
Pipeline cleanliness
Video quality
Brand consistency
A/B variant usefulness
Revision speed
Publishable score
Workflow reusability
Technical execution
```

## Final positioning

Use this sentence in the product pitch:

> A TRAE-orchestrated AI content factory that turns a marketing brief and brand kit into two publish-ready short-form video variants in under 60 minutes.
